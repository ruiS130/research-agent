# Research Brief: Solid-State Battery Commercial Barriers (2024–2025)

## Summary
Solid-state batteries (SSBs) are moving from research toward commercialization, with a likely path that starts in consumer electronics and later reaches electric vehicles (EVs) [1]. Current barriers cluster around electrolyte selection, interface stability, manufacturing scale-up, and cost [1][2].

## Key Findings

**Staged commercialization path.** Analysts describe a sequence in which SSBs first enter smaller consumer devices and then expand to EVs as the technology matures [1].

**Electrolyte choice is a central decision.** Solid electrolytes fall into several main classes, each with different performance and processing trade-offs. Selecting among them shapes the whole commercialization timeline [1][2].

**Interface stability is the near-term hurdle.** A 2025 perspective frames the path to industrial uptake in two steps: short-term improvements in interface stability, followed by longer-term work toward mass-market adoption [2].

**Manufacturing and cost remain unresolved.** Scaling production from lab to factory and reducing cost are identified as major barriers to commercial deployment [1].
